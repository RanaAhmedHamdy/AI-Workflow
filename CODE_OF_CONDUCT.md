# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and inclusive community. Be constructive, assume good intent, give technical feedback about ideas and evidence rather than people, and do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or publish private information.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) with “Code of Conduct” in the subject. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.
